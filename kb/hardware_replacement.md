# Knowledge Base: Laptop and Peripheral Hardware Replacement Procedure

**Category:** Hardware  
**Tags:** hardware, laptop, battery, keyboard, replacement, warranty, asset, broken, peripheral  
**Applicability:** Corporate laptops, docks, chargers, and standard peripherals

---

## Overview
IT Operations maintains an active inventory of standard hardware assets to support rapid replacement for hardware failures, physical damage, or battery degradation.

## Replacement Eligibility Criteria
1. **Battery Health:**
   - On Windows: Run `powercfg /batteryreport`. If full charge capacity is below 70% of design capacity, replacement is approved.
   - On macOS: Check **System Settings > Battery > Battery Health**. If status indicates "Service Recommended", replacement is approved.
2. **Physical / Component Failure:**
   - Swollen chassis, trackpad bulging (immediate critical hazard; user must disconnect and power off immediately).
   - Non-functioning display, keyboard keys, or charging port failure confirmed by hardware diagnostics.
3. **Hardware Lifecycle:**
   - Standard refresh cycle is 36 months from initial device provisioning.

## Replacement Workflow
1. **Remote Employees:**
   - IT dispatches an expedited replacement laptop pre-enrolled with standard image.
   - A prepaid return shipping label and foam return box are included in the package.
   - The user has 14 calendar days to transfer personal local files and ship back the old unit.
2. **On-Site Employees:**
   - Visit the IT Service Bar in Building 4, 2nd Floor, between 09:00 and 17:00 local time for immediate same-day swap.
3. **Peripherals (Monitors, Keyboards, Mice, Headsets):**
   - Standard ergonomic peripherals can be ordered directly from the IT Hardware Depot on the intranet without returning older items unless defective.
