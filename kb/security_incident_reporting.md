# Knowledge Base: Security Incident and Phishing Reporting Protocol

**Category:** Access  
**Tags:** security, phishing, incident, compromise, access, malware, virus, critical  
**Applicability:** All company systems, laptops, emails, and cloud accounts

---

## CRITICAL INCIDENT PROTOCOL
> **IMMEDIATE ACTION REQUIRED:**  
> If you suspect malware execution, unauthorized account activity, or entered credentials into an unrecognized external link:
> 1. **Disconnect from all networks immediately** (unplug Ethernet cable and turn off Wi-Fi).
> 2. **DO NOT power off or reboot the machine** (rebooting destroys volatile RAM forensics needed by the Security Operations Center).
> 3. Call the 24/7 Security Hotline immediately at extension **5555** or dial `+1-800-555-SECU`.

## Phishing and Suspicious Email Handling
1. If an email seems fraudulent (requests gift cards, urgent wire transfers, or asks you to verify your password):
   - In Outlook, click the **Report Phishing** (PhishAlarm) hook button in the ribbon.
   - Do not forward the message manually to colleagues.
   - Do not click links or download any attachments.
2. If you clicked a link and submitted credentials:
   - Report the incident immediately to `security-incident@internal.corp`.
   - Your account will be placed into high-alert containment and your active sessions revoked.

## Escalation and SLA
- All tickets marked as **Security Incident** or **Compromise** are assigned **Critical** priority.
- The Security Operations Center (SOC) SLA for first response is **15 minutes**.
- Automated triage agents must route these tickets to human security analysts without auto-closing.
